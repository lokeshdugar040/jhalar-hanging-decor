3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We share it only:

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. Enquiry data is retained only as long as needed for our business relationship or as required by law (e.g. GST records). No transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

5. Your Rights
