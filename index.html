About JHALAR

JHALAR is a Howrah, West Bengal workshop specialising in handcrafted artificial flower jhalars and hanging décor. We combine traditional garland craft with durable modern materials so every piece stays vibrant, event after event.

As a direct manufacturer we serve business buyers — event decorators, wholesalers, retailers and organisations — with honest pricing, consistent finishing and the flexibility to customise.

  • Handcrafted in-house, quality-checked per piece
  • Reusable artificial flowers, beads & brass-finish components
  • B2B-first: quotations, packing & dispatch handled professionally
