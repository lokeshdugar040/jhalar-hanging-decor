About JHALAR

A Workshop Dedicated to Hanging Décor

JHALAR is a Howrah-based (West Bengal) workshop specialising in one thing: handcrafted hanging decorations. Every garland is made by artisans who have perfected the twist, knot and bloom placement that gives a jhalar its fullness.

We supply business buyers only — decorators, wholesalers, retailers and organisations — which keeps our pricing direct and our quality repeatable.

  • Artisan-made with consistent, repeatable quality
  • Designed for bulk production and safe transit
  • Custom colours, sizes and private-label options
