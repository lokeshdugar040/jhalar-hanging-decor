# 📞 How to Update Your Contact Information

## Step 1: Open index.html

Open the file `index.html` in a text editor (like Notepad, VS Code, or any text editor).

## Step 2: Find and Replace YOUR_NUMBER_HERE

Press `Ctrl+F` (or `Cmd+F` on Mac) to search.

Search for: `YOUR_NUMBER_HERE`

You'll find it in 5 places:

1. **Line ~87** - Hero section WhatsApp button
2. **Line ~234** - CTA section WhatsApp button  
3. **Line ~327** - Contact card (display number)
4. **Line ~368** - Footer contact info
5. **Line ~380** - Footer WhatsApp link
6. **Line ~402** - Product modal WhatsApp button

## Step 3: Replace with YOUR Number

Replace `YOUR_NUMBER_HERE` with your actual WhatsApp number.

**Format:** Country code + your number (NO + symbol, NO spaces, NO dashes)

### Example:
If your number is: +91 98765 43210

Replace with: `919876543210`

**NOT:** `+919876543210` ❌  
**NOT:** `91 98765 43210` ❌  
**NOT:** `91-98765-43210` ❌  
**YES:** `919876543210` ✅

## Step 4: Update Email

Search for: `your-email@gmail.com`

Replace with your actual business email.

Example: `contact@jhalar.com` or `lokesh@yourbusiness.com`

## Step 5: Save the File

Press `Ctrl+S` (or `Cmd+S` on Mac) to save.

## Step 6: Commit Changes to GitHub

1. Go to your repository: https://github.com/lokeshdugar040/jhalar-hanging-decor
2. Click "index.html"
3. Click the pencil icon (Edit)
4. Scroll down and click "Commit changes"
5. Add a message like "Update contact info"
6. Click "Commit changes"

## Done! ✅

Your website will update automatically in 1-2 minutes.
